Standards & Conduct

Server Rules & Guidelines

1. No Random Deathmatch (RDM)

Do not harm or attack other players without prior verbal initiation and a clear, realistic motive.

2. No Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

Never deliberately ram vehicles into pedestrians or other cars without authorized pursuit maneuvers.

3. Fear RP & Value of Life

Value your character's life. When held at gunpoint or significantly outnumbered, reasonably comply with demands.
