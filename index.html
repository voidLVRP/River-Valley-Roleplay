Standards & Regulations

Community Server Rules

Adherence to our roleplay standards ensures a quality experience for all citizens.

•

No Random Deathmatch (RDM)

Do not harm or execute another player without a valid, pre-established roleplay interaction and proper initiation.

•

No Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

Never deliberately ram your vehicle into pedestrians or other vehicles without realistic high-speed pursuit PIT authorization.

•

Fear RP & Value of Life

You must value your character life. When outnumbered or held at gunpoint with no immediate escape, follow commands reasonably.

•

New Life Rule (NLR)

Upon respawning after death, you forget the past events that led to your demise and cannot return to the scene for 15 minutes.

•

Priority & Cooldown Times

Major crimes (Bank robbery, jewelry heist, hostage scenarios) require active Peace Officer supervisor presence and a 20-min cooldown.

•

No Fail RP / Unrealistic Driving

Supercars driving off-road over mountain cliffs or driving on shredded tires is strictly prohibited. Keep driving physics realistic.
