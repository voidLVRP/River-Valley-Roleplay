Standards & Conduct

Server Rules & Guidelines

Compliance with community standards is mandatory for all citizens and first responders.

1. No Random Deathmatch (RDM)

Do not harm, attack, or execute other players without prior verbal/roleplay initiation and a clear, realistic motive.

2. No Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

Never deliberately ram your vehicle into pedestrians or other cars without authorized pursuit PIT maneuvers.

3. Fear RP & Value of Life

Value your character's life. When held at gunpoint or significantly outnumbered, reasonably comply with demands.

4. New Life Rule (NLR)

Upon respawning after death, you forget all events leading to your demise and cannot return to that scene for 15 minutes.

5. Unrealistic Driving & Off-Roading

Supercars and low-clearance vehicles cannot drive over steep mountain rockfaces or off-road trails. Stay on asphalt and designated dirt roads.

6. Priority & Robbery Cooldowns

Major crimes (Bank robbery, jewelry store heist, hostage standoff) require active supervisor presence and a 20-minute cooldown.
